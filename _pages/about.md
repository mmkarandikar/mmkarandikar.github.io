Hello, my name is <b>Mandar</b>. I work as a Scienfitic Coordinator for the Excellence Cluster -- [Dynaverse](https://dynaverse.astro.uni-koeln.de/) at the University of Bonn. My background lies in theoretical modelling, data science, and predictive machine learning algorithms. My research focus has been on modelling the formation of structure in the Universe. In July 2025, completed my PhD in Astrophysics at the [Argelander-Institute for Astronomy](https://astro.uni-bonn.de/en) as part of the [Large-scale structure and Cosmology research group](https://astro.uni-bonn.de/~porciani/lss/webpage/index.html).
